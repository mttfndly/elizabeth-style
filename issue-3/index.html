Let’s dive in.Why Green Belongs in Your WardrobeGreen is that rare hue that flatters everyone—offering balance, depth, and a touchof unexpected luxury. FRIENDSHIP GLOSS NOTEJOY IS STILL FOUND IN THESMALL MOMENTS AND IN THE STILL.THE ART OF FRIENDSHIP:When Your Friend Feels the Winter BluesWinter can be beautiful, but let’s be honest — it also brings a stillness that sometimessettles into sadness.The days are shorter, the energy dips, and even the brightest souls can feel a littledimmed.If someone you care about is going through a heavy season, you can be their softlanding — their quiet reminder that they’re not alone.What to Say: Gentle Words That Reach the Heart“I’ve noticed you’ve been a little quiet — how are you, really?”“You don’t have to carry this alone. I’m here — even if we just sit in silence.”“It’s okay not to be okay. You’re doing better than you realise.”“Would it be okay if I check in later this week — just to see how you're going?”What to Do: Small Gestures That Mean EverythingInvite them into warmth. A slow walk, a low-pressure movie night, or a quiet coffee catch-up —simple, cosy moments speak volumes.Send a surprise. A handwritten note, their favourite snack, a candle that smells like sunshine.Tiny things, big love.Offer practical help. “Want me to fold laundry while we chat?” or “I’ll drop dinner off Tuesday.”Acts like these go deeper than words.Just show up. Even if they say “I’m fine,” your presence can be the calm in their storm.Create a gentle ritual. A weekly check-in text. A Friday afternoon voice note. Somethingconsistent, something real.Be real too. Share your own ups and downs. Vulnerability builds the bridge.Celebrate the small wins. “You made it to the gym.” “I saw you spending time with your kids.”Tiny moments deserve applause.Go with them. If they need extra help, offer to sit in the GP’s waiting room. That’s love in action.Final Thought: Be the soft PlaceFriendship in winter isn’t about fixing — it’s about making space. To feel. To be seen. To not beforgotten.Your quiet presence can be the difference between feeling alone and feeling held.Send the text. Drop the coffee. Sit in the silence.Sometimes, that’s everything.
